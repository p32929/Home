# Chrome Web Store listing for Home - Clean New Tab

Upload `Home.zip` from this folder (or the latest release) in the Chrome Web Store developer dashboard.

## Short description (under 132 characters)

A clean new tab page with a clock, a search box and your favourite sites one click away. Your links stay on your device.

## Long description

Home replaces your new tab with a calm, clean page.

1. A big clock and a friendly greeting.
2. A search box that uses your browser's search engine, or opens an address you type.
3. Your favourite sites as tidy tiles with their own icons. Add, edit, delete and drag to reorder.
4. One click on the toolbar button adds the page you are on.
5. Light, dark or follow your system.
6. Export your links to a file and import them on another computer.

Free and open source: https://github.com/p32929/Home

## Privacy

Your links are stored only in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Permission reasons for the review form:
1. storage: saves your links in your browser.
2. activeTab: reads the title and address of the current tab, only when you click the toolbar button to add it.
3. favicon: shows each site's icon from your browser's own icon cache, without loading anything from the internet.
4. search: sends what you type in the search box to your browser's default search engine.
